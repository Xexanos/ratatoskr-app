This version needs Ratatoskr server 2.0.0 or newer and asks for a one-time sign-in after the update - your server, certificate and username carry over, so only the password is needed. In exchange the app stays signed in until you sign out: it holds its own sign-in with Ratatoskr instead of passing your media-server session along, so server restarts and long pauses no longer sign you out. Signing out now tells the server too, and a server too old for this app says so.
